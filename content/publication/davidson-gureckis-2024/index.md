abstract: Goals play a central role in the study of agentic behavior.  But what is
  a goal, and how should we best represent them?  The traditional reinforcement learning
  answer is that all goals are expressible as the maximization of future rewards.  While
  parsimonious, such a definition seems insufficient when viewed from both the perspective
  of humans specifying goals to machines and autotelic agents that self-propose tasks
  to explore and learn.  We offer a critical perspective on the distillation of all
  goals directly into reward functions.  We identify key features we believe goal
  representations ought to have, and then offer a proposal we believe meets those
  considerations
